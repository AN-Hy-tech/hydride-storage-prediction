## What these numbers are a claim about

Every row of the tables below is an **out-of-fold** reading: each training row was predicted by the outer fold that held it out, so the score is honest, but it comes from five different models. It is a claim about the *procedure*, not about the model that ships. The artifact reading — one promoted model on rows it has never seen — is a claim about *a model*, and it arrives with card #34.

> **The interval is too narrow: the out-of-fold predictions come from five models with overlapping training sets, so K-fold cross-validation has no unbiased variance estimator (Bengio & Grandvalet 2004); this prices the sampling of materials, not of folds.**

A second, cruder interval over the five fold scores rides beside it in the same table, under `interval_from = per-fold scores`. It prices fold-to-fold spread — part of which is training size rather than the model, because screening removes between 15 and 144 of a fold's ~710 rows. The two intervals will disagree. That is the honest state of the estimate.
